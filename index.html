The workflow

Built around the actual clip.

01 — SEARCH

Find relevant gameplay

Explore supported Twitch and Kick clips, including both English- and Spanish-speaking creators, subject to platform availability and permissions.

02 — ANALYZE

Understand the highlight

Audio transcription, frame sampling, and AI-assisted analysis help identify what happened and propose an accurate headline.

03 — PREPARE

Make it vertical

Prepare a short-form edit, add headline narration and attribution, check content-safety flags, and generate an editable caption.

Account connection

Your account stays yours.

TikTok authorization is requested through its official authentication process, not through this website.

Opt-in accessThe desktop app requests only the permissions needed for draft upload or approved direct publishing.
Local token storageAuthorization tokens and settings are stored on the user's computer, not in this static website.
Creator controlReview clips, respect content rights, and confirm approvals before public posting. TikTok may restrict publishing based on its own review processes.

Responsible use

Credits and permissions are essential.

Clips may contain content created by independent streamers. GTA6 AutoClips does not grant the right to reuse someone else's recording. Users are responsible for obtaining appropriate permissions, respecting platform rules and checking generated titles and moderation results.

AI can make mistakes. Visual and audio filters cannot guarantee that every clip is suitable for every platform.
